# Public release status

Checked 2026-11-03.

The private LaMP-3 human audit used 60 users, 30 calibration users and 30 held-out test users, with eight candidate records per user and two independent annotation passes. The completed private report has 480 audited records, 31 category-set disagreements resolved by adjudication, 97.08% agreement on the any-disclosure label, and Cohen's kappa 0.883 for that label.

The human-calibrated operating point did not change the selected grid index at alpha 0.2 or 0.5 in this small cohort. At alpha 0.2 the selected rule admitted zero records on the held-out users; at alpha 0.5 the held-out human exposure mean was 0.3667 with four admitted records on average. These are descriptive results for this LaMP-3 sample, not population estimates and not validation of the other tasks.

The public repository contains only the aggregate human-audit summary. Raw review text, row-level human labels, model labels, private keys, and annotation packets remain private.
